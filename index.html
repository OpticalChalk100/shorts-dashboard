How the YouTube Shorts algorithm works (In Simple Terms)

When you upload a new Short, YouTube doesn't blast it immediately. Its AI tests the video with a small sample audience usually 6 to 24 hours after upload. If viewers watch all the way through without swiping away (which our high-speed +13% pacing and meme popups are engineered for), the algorithm pushes it out into the viral Shorts feed!
